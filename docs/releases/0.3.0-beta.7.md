# Velocity Type 0.3.0-beta.7 — Public Beta

**Status:** Beta version for testing. It may contain bugs and receive further adjustments.

## Highlights

- Brand-new menus: Play now, Custom race, Garage, History, Progression and Settings.
- Play now starts a race in one click with your saved setup.
- Garage with paint preview and level unlocks; history table; daily goals with reset timer.
- Settings: volume, sound, mistake mode, typing rules, language, pilot name, reduce motion and visual quality.
- Everything from previous betas: English/Portuguese UI, CrazyGames-ready build, camera follow, turbo, strong mistake feedback.
